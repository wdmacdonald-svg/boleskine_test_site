Legacy of the Caman

Boleskine Camanachd Club has been a cornerstone of the Stratherrick, Foyers and Strathnairn community and the wider shinty world for nearly a century.

Based in Inverarnie near Farr, Inverness-shire, we pride ourselves on preserving the traditions of Scotland's most ancient sport. From the generations of players who have wielded the caman in our hooped black and gold jersey, our legacy is one of resilience, community pride, and passion.

As we look out across the Highlands, we play not just for the game but for the heritage of our land and a hopeful, bright future for shinty in our community.
